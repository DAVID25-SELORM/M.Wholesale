## Who holds the sensitive rights

- **invite users** (`users.invite`): GENERAL_MANAGER, OWNER, SUPER_ADMIN
- **assign roles** (`roles.assign`): GENERAL_MANAGER, OWNER, SUPER_ADMIN
- **read the audit log** (`audit.view`): AUDITOR, BRANCH_MANAGER, GENERAL_MANAGER, OWNER, SUPER_ADMIN
- **see cost prices and margins** (`reports.margins`): ACCOUNTS_MANAGER, AUDITOR, BRANCH_MANAGER, GENERAL_MANAGER, OWNER, SALES_MANAGER, SUPER_ADMIN
- **see stock valuation** (`inventory.valuation`): ACCOUNTS_MANAGER, ACCOUNTS_OFFICER, AUDITOR, GENERAL_MANAGER, OWNER, PROCUREMENT_MANAGER, SUPER_ADMIN
- **override credit limits** (`sales.override_credit`): ACCOUNTS_MANAGER, GENERAL_MANAGER, OWNER, SUPER_ADMIN
- **reverse invoices** (`sales.reverse`): ACCOUNTS_MANAGER, GENERAL_MANAGER, OWNER, SUPER_ADMIN
- **reverse credit notes** (`credit_notes.reverse`): ACCOUNTS_MANAGER, GENERAL_MANAGER, OWNER, SUPER_ADMIN
- **pay customers back** (`refunds.create`): ACCOUNTS_MANAGER, BRANCH_MANAGER, GENERAL_MANAGER, OWNER, SALES_MANAGER, SUPER_ADMIN
- **void customer payments** (`receipts.void`): ACCOUNTS_MANAGER, GENERAL_MANAGER, OWNER, SUPER_ADMIN
- **adjust stock** (`inventory.adjust`): GENERAL_MANAGER, OWNER, SUPER_ADMIN, WAREHOUSE_MANAGER
- **read the controlled-drugs register** (`registers.controlled`): AUDITOR, BRANCH_MANAGER, GENERAL_MANAGER, OWNER, PHARMACIST, SUPER_ADMIN, WAREHOUSE_MANAGER
- **close other cashiers' till shifts** (`pos.manage`): BRANCH_MANAGER, GENERAL_MANAGER, OWNER, SALES_MANAGER, SUPER_ADMIN
